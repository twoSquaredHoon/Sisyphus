Wandering Thoughts

Some mornings the world feels like a blank page, waiting patiently for something to happen. Coffee steams on the table, the street outside is quiet, and for a moment, nothing needs to be done.

It's easy to forget how much beauty hides in ordinary things — the way light slants through a window, the hum of a refrigerator, the small rituals that hold a day together.

Maybe that's the trick to a good life: not chasing bigger moments, but noticing the ones already here.
